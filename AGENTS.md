# Project Design Guidelines: Sterling Mangoes

## Typography & Branding
- **Logo Style**: Restored to the original nature-themed leaf logo with the Sterling text.
- **Logotype Styling**: Green primary color. No longer completely ALL CAPS Christian Dior.
- **General Vibe**: Premium, heritage, direct-to-consumer luxury produce (mangoes).

## Colors
- Parchment/off-white backgrounds
- High contrast ink (black) and white
- Accents of mango and leaf green
